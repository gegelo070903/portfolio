The Team

Meet my AI team

Five sidechats, five roles. Each one looked at the same goal from a different angle.

Designer

Make joining look good

Every new member gets a welcome card with their photo and their new website link, made to share. When a member shares it, E2 gets seen by all their friends for free.

Developer

Turn member sites into invites

Add one small line to the bottom of every free member website: "Made at E2, get yours free." Hundreds of member sites are already out there working as quiet invites, every single day.

Marketing

Show it, do not just say it

Post short 30 second videos of a real member getting their website built live. Filipino freelancers and designers watch TikTok, Reels, and Shorts, so that is where E2 should be seen.

Community Manager

Give every newcomer a buddy

Pair each new member with one older member for their first week. People who feel noticed in week one stay, help others later, and invite their friends themselves.

Content Creator

Tell one member story a week

A short post about one real member: where they started, what they built, what changed. Real stories beat ads, and every story gives that member a reason to share E2 with pride.

All five together

One simple loop

A new member joins, gets a site, gets a welcome card, shares both, and brings the next member in. Growth that feeds itself, without buying ads.

The Plan

The big 5 growth ideas

If E2 only did these five things, the team believes 235 new members in 90 days is realistic.

1

A share line on every member website

Every free site Sam builds carries a small "Made at E2" line that links back to the join page. Members already share their sites everywhere. Each share becomes an invitation that never stops working.

2

Welcome cards people want to post

When a member joins and their site goes live, they receive a nice welcome card with their name, photo, and site link. Most people will post it on Facebook or X the same day. That is free reach from the happiest moment of joining.

3

Live build videos, twice a week

Short clips showing a website going from an intro post to a live page in minutes. End every clip the same way: "Yours is free, link in bio." Post them where Filipino developers, designers, and virtual assistants already spend time.

4

A public bounty board

Show open bounties, like this one, on a public page outside Discord. When people outside can see real tasks with real peso rewards, joining stops being a promise and becomes a clear next step.

5

Bring a friend weeks

Once a month, run one friendly invite week. Members invite people they actually know, new guests get the full welcome, and the members who brought them get thanked in the weekly wins post. Recognition, not pressure.

The Culture

Keeping it the friendliest

Growth only works if the room stays kind. These are the community rules my team kept coming back to.

No dumb questions, ever

Beginners ask basic questions in every community. In E2, the first reply is always helpful, never mocking. That one habit is what new members remember and tell others about.

Feedback starts with a strength

When reviewing someone's work, name one thing that works before one thing to fix. Honest, but kind. People improve faster and keep showing their work.

Notice people in public

Weekly wins, first client shoutouts, and small tips for good work. People stay in rooms where they are seen, and they invite friends to rooms that treat them well.

Check in, do not chase

If a member goes quiet for two weeks, one friendly message: "We miss your work, everything okay?" No sales, no guilt. Just care. Many will come back on their own.

The Roadmap

A simple 3 step plan

Nothing here needs a budget. It needs a few weeks of consistency.

Weeks 1 to 2

Build the loop

Add the share line to member sites, make the welcome card, and put a live member counter on e2.life showing the road to 1,000.

Weeks 3 to 6

Start the content engine

Two live build clips and one member story every week, posted on TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and in Filipino freelancer groups.

Weeks 7 to 12

Close the gap together

Run bring a friend weeks, publish the public bounty board, and count down to 1,000 as a whole community, not a sales target.

What I will do myself

I am John, a web developer and designer from the Philippines. I will share my own E2 site, post my work on Show Your Work Fridays, and welcome new Filipino members who arrive in my time zone. Small help, every week, from every member, is how a friendly room reaches 1,000.
